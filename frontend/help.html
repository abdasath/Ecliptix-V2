Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download my games?

After purchasing, your games will instantly appear in your Library. You can download the DRM-free installer directly from there without any extra launcher required.

Are my payments secure?

Yes! We use Razorpay to process all transactions securely. Ecliptix never stores your credit card or UPI details on our servers.

Can I refund a game?

We offer a no-questions-asked 14-day refund policy for games played less than 2 hours. Simply submit a ticket below.

Do you sell Ecliptix Gift Cards?

Yes, digital gift cards will be launching later this year. You can use them to add funds directly to your wallet.

Is Ecliptix available globally?

Absolutely. You can purchase and play our DRM-free games from anywhere in the world. Pricing will automatically adjust to your local currency.
